I am an assistant professor of economics at [Grand Valley State University](https://www.gvsu.edu/seidman/form/6e5d5f89-fb66-4eb5-9eb2-50e1ccf7aed6/response/74be0f4e-a3fe-42da-8060-380457d75723).

My research focuses on how households form expectations, specifically about inflation, and its implications and insights for monetary policymakers. Additionally, I am co-author of the monthly [Purchasing Manager's Index for West Michigan](https://www.gvsu.edu/supplychainmgt/purchasing-managers-index-survey-2.htm).

I earned my Ph.D. in economics from Texas A&M University in May 2024. Aligned with my research interests, I teach undergraduate macroeconomics as well as the economics capstone course. Before my doctorate, I earned a B.S. in economics from Purdue University, an M.S. in economics from the University of Wisconsin–Madison, and spent time as a research economist for the White House Council of Economic Advisers.
